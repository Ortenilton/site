About me

Hi! I am Ortenilton, a Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at the University of Coimbra, under the joint supervision of Edgard Pimentel and Ercília Sousa. My research focuses on the analysis of partial differential equations, particularly on the theory of viscosity solutions. My current work is centered on fractional vanishing viscosity and the numerical methods for related problems.

I hold a Master's degree in Mathematics from PUC-Rio (Brazil, 2023), advised by Edgard Pimentel and Gustavo Araújo. During my master's studies, I was funded through FAPERJ's Mestrado Nota 10 scholarship, a highly selective funding program.

Before that, I completed my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics at the Federal University of Sergipe (Brazil, 2021). During my undergraduate studies, I participated three times in the Institutional Program for Scientific Initiation Scholarships (PIBIC) and once in the Institutional Program for Teaching Initiation Scholarships (PIBID).

Research

My research focuses on the analysis of nonlinear partial differential equations, particularly Hamilton–Jacobi equations and the theory of viscosity solutions. My current interests include vanishing viscosity methods, nonlocal and fractional equations, regularity theory, and the development and analysis of numerical schemes for nonlinear PDEs.

  • Viscosity solutions
  • Hamilton–Jacobi equations
  • Vanishing viscosity
  • Nonlocal & fractional equations
  • Regularity theory
  • Numerical schemes for nonlinear PDEs
